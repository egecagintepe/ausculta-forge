## Week-3 Module B Decision Summary

- Board candidate identity is closed as ESP32-S3-DevKitC-1-N8R8; live stock/revision and two data-cable readiness remain procurement checks.
- Wired Native USB is the selected physical medium only. CDC-ACM versus Vendor Bulk remains open.
- 48 kHz mono is the nominal baseline, not yet a frozen acquisition profile.
- The B-to-C frame is conceptual only. Exact offsets, frame size, buffer size, clock/timestamp units, sample packing and final byte order remain Week-4 freeze items.
- DW03-09 is jointly owned by Module B and Module C and remains OPEN until both owners agree; it must not be closed unilaterally.
- ECM digitalisation remains OPEN. ADC1 is a bench candidate; PCM1808 is only an advisor-approved external-ADC candidate. The PCM1808 purchase hold does not block independently ready Group-A items.
- The current source BOM plans 3 × EB-R and 2 × PAM8403, with long-lead-time, power/pad-map, live-price/stock and amplifier/12 Ω compatibility risks explicitly open.
- No physical measurements or hardware tests are claimed by this document-only closeout.
