## A
* **AOP (Acoustic Overload Point):** The sound pressure level at which an acoustic microphone diaphragm or preamplifier outputs 10% Total Harmonic Distortion (THD). For the INMP441, AOP is $120 \text{ dB SPL}$.
* **Analytic Signal:** A complex-valued time-domain signal $z(t) = x(t) + j\mathcal{H}\{x(t)\}$ formed by combining the real signal with its Hilbert transform, having zero spectral power at negative frequencies.
* **Anti-Aliasing Filter:** An analog or digital low-pass filter attenuating frequency components above the Nyquist frequency ($f_s / 2$) prior to sampling.
* **Arbitrary Delay Vector ($z_i$):** The internal delay line memory state preserved in an IIR digital filter between contiguous blocks.
* **ASGI (Asynchronous Server Gateway Interface):** The modern Python specification for asynchronous web servers (e.g. Uvicorn/FastAPI).

---

## B
* **BCLK (Bit Clock / SCK):** The continuous serial clock line on an I2S bus driving individual bit transitions. In AuscultaForge, $f_{\text{SCK}} = 3.072 \text{ MHz}$.
* **Biquad (Biquadratic Filter):** A second-order recursive digital filter section having two poles and two zeros in the $z$-domain.
* **Bit Depth:** The number of binary bits allocated to represent the amplitude of a discrete audio sample (e.g. 16, 24, or 32 bits).
* **Boxcar Window:** A rectangular time window having uniform gain ($1.0$) across its duration and zero elsewhere.
* **Buffer Backpressure:** The condition where a data producer generates samples faster than a consumer can process them, causing queue saturation.

---

## C
* **Causal Filter:** A digital filter whose output $y[n]$ depends solely on current and past input samples ($x[n], x[n-1], \dots$) and past outputs ($y[n-1], \dots$), never on future inputs.
* **CDC-ACM (Communication Device Class — Abstract Control Model):** A standard USB class protocol exposing virtual serial communication ports (`COMx`) over USB.
* **CirCor DigiScope Dataset:** An open-access pediatric phonocardiogram database provided by PhysioNet (Challenge 2022) with physician-annotated heart sound boundaries.
* **Coherence ($\gamma_{xy}^2(f)$):** A normalized frequency-domain metric ($0 \le \gamma^2 \le 1$) measuring the degree of linear relationship between an input stimulus and output capture.
* **Crest Factor:** The ratio of the maximum absolute peak amplitude to the effective RMS level of a signal ($C = x_{\text{peak}} / x_{\text{RMS}}$).

---

## D
* **dBFS (Decibels relative to Full Scale):** A logarithmic unit of amplitude where $0 \text{ dBFS}$ represents the maximum numerical peak representable by the digital data format.
* **Direct Memory Access (DMA):** A dedicated hardware controller that transfers data directly between a peripheral (e.g. I2S) and memory (SRAM) without CPU intervention.
* **Display Decimator:** An algorithm that compresses high-rate audio streams into a bounded envelope vector ($\le 600$ points) preserving transient peaks for 60 FPS web rendering.
* **DTR (Data Terminal Ready):** A virtual modem control line in USB CDC-ACM asserted by the host PC when opening a communication port.

---

## E
* **ECM (Electret Condenser Microphone):** An electrostatic acoustic transducer utilizing a permanently polarized electret diaphragm and internal JFET impedance converter.
* **Endianness:** The sequential order in which bytes of a multi-byte word are stored in computer memory (Little-Endian vs. Big-Endian).

---

## F
* **$F_1$-Score:** The harmonic mean of precision and recall ($F_1 = 2 \frac{P \cdot R}{P + R}$), providing a balanced performance metric under class imbalance.
* **FastAPI:** A modern, high-performance Python web framework built on Starlette and Pydantic.
* **FreeRTOS:** A real-time operating system kernel providing deterministic preemptive multitasking for microcontrollers.

---

## G
* **Gabor Limit:** The fundamental time-frequency uncertainty inequality $\Delta t \cdot \Delta f \ge 1/4\pi$.
* **Golden Vectors:** Deterministic binary and tabular test fixtures generated by a reference C program to verify bit-exact parity in downstream software.
* **Group Delay ($\tau_g$):** The rate of change of total filter phase shift with respect to angular frequency ($-\frac{d\phi}{d\omega}$).

---

## H
* **$H_1(f)$ Transfer Function:** A Single-Input Single-Output (SISO) frequency response estimator ($H_1 = S_{xy} / S_{xx}$) that cancels output observation noise.
* **Hann (Hanning) Window:** A raised cosine window $w[n] = 0.5(1 - \cos(2\pi n / (N-1)))$ providing $-18 \text{ dB/octave}$ sidelobe roll-off.
* **Hilbert Transform:** A linear operator shifting all positive frequency components by $-90^\circ$, used to construct complex analytic signals.
* **HSMM (Hidden Semi-Markov Model):** A statistical sequential model explicitly parameterizing state duration distributions, avoiding exponential duration assumptions.

---

## I
* **I2S (Inter-IC Sound):** A synchronous serial electrical bus interface standard designed by Philips for transmitting digital audio between integrated circuits.
* **INMP441:** An omnidirectional bottom-port digital MEMS microphone with integrated 24-bit $\Sigma\Delta$ ADC and standard I2S output.

---

## L
* **Least-Squares Gain ($\hat{g}_{\text{LS}}$):** The scalar path gain minimizing the squared error between an aligned stimulus and response ($\sum x y / \sum x^2$).
* **LR-HSMM:** Logistic Regression-based Hidden Semi-Markov Model, combining discriminative logistic emission models with semi-Markovian state transitions.

---

## M
* **Mains Interference:** Electromagnetic hum coupled into audio sensors at power line frequencies ($50 \text{ Hz}$ in Europe/Turkey, $60 \text{ Hz}$ in the Americas) and integer harmonics.
* **MEMS (Micro-Electro-Mechanical Systems):** Microscopic mechanical diaphragms and sensor structures etched directly onto silicon wafers alongside CMOS circuitry.
* **Monotonic Sequence Counter:** An unsigned integer incrementing strictly by one for each transmitted packet, used to detect packet drops and gaps.

---

## N
* **Nyquist Frequency ($f_N$):** Half of the continuous sampling frequency ($f_s / 2$). The maximum frequency that can be represented without aliasing.

---

## P
* **PCG (Phonocardiography):** The graphic recording and digital acoustic analysis of cardiac heart sounds and murmurs ($20\text{--}500\text{ Hz}$).
* **PSRAM (Pseudo-Static RAM):** High-density external RAM combining DRAM storage cells with an internal refresh controller, accessed over SPI.

---

## R
* **RIFF (Resource Interchange File Format):** The container chunk structure governing standard uncompressed Microsoft WAV audio files.
* **Rolling Buffer:** A thread-safe circular memory ring that continuously retains a fixed duration of the most recent audio samples (e.g. 5.0 seconds).

---

## S
* **$S_1$ (First Heart Sound):** Low-frequency ($20\text{--}150\text{ Hz}$), $100\text{--}150\text{ ms}$ sound produced by the closure of the mitral and tricuspid valves.
* **$S_2$ (Second Heart Sound):** Higher-frequency ($50\text{--}250\text{ Hz}$), $80\text{--}120\text{ ms}$ sound produced by the closure of the aortic and pulmonic valves.
* **`S32_LE`:** 32-bit signed integer in Little-Endian byte order.
* **SampleBlock:** The universal immutable dataclass container holding an audio chunk, sampling rate, arrival timestamp, and sequence number.
* **Schmidt Spike Removal:** A non-linear filtering technique attenuating impulse noise spikes exceeding three times the median of window maxima.
* **Second-Order Sections (SOS):** Cascaded second-order digital filter biquads used to maintain numerical stability in IIR filtering.
* **SHA-256:** A 256-bit cryptographic secure hash algorithm used in AuscultaForge to guarantee immutable session provenance.
* **Sign Extension:** Replicating the sign bit of an $N$-bit two's-complement integer across the upper bits of an $M$-bit container ($M > N$) to preserve negative values.
* **Spectral Leakage:** Smeared frequency components resulting from truncating continuous signals with finite rectangular windows.
* **STFT (Short-Time Fourier Transform):** Sliding-window discrete Fourier transform generating 2D time-frequency spectrograms.

---

## T
* **Teager-Kaiser Energy Operator (TKEO):** The non-linear operator $\Psi[x[n]] = x^2[n] - x[n-1]x[n+1]$, proportional to amplitude squared and frequency squared.
* **TinyUSB:** An open-source, cross-platform USB host/device stack supporting USB CDC-ACM on the ESP32-S3.
* **Two's Complement:** The universal binary representation for signed integers where the most significant bit has negative weight ($-2^{N-1}$).

---

## V
* **Viterbi Algorithm:** A dynamic programming algorithm finding the most probable sequence of hidden states in an HMM/HSMM trellis.
* **Virtual Bench:** A deterministic test suite simulating 8 mathematical known-answer scenarios for full-pipeline verification without physical hardware.

---

## W
* **Welch Method:** A low-variance spectral density estimation technique averaging modified periodograms from overlapping windowed segments.
* **WS (Word Select / LRCLK):** The I2S clock line indicating whether data on the serial data line belongs to the Left channel (`WS = 0`) or Right channel (`WS = 1`).
